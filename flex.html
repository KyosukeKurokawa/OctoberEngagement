Why do you need to have an accessible website? What does that mean?

An accessible website is designed and developed so that people with disabilities can perceive, understand, navigate, and interact with it effectively. Moreover, the primary goal of the web is universal access, regardless of physical or cognitive ability. Accessibility ensures no group of users is arbitrarily locked out of digital services, education, commerce, or communication.
